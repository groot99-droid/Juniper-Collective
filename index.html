What We Do

Most firms handle one piece of the work: the brand, the build, the launch, the party. Juniper holds the whole arc. We start with the question “what should this become, and for whom?” and carry it through identity, pricing, audience, pre-launch marketing, and the opening experience itself.

Phase I

Conception & Positioning

Defining the core commercial ethos, property-level differentiation, and initial architectural thesis before ground is broken.

Phase II

Identity & Pricing Architecture

Structuring commercial yield modeling, bespoke visual systems, spatial vernacular, and tiered guest segmentation.

Phase III

Pre-Launch Demand

Generating private interest lists, editorial narrative dissemination, founder salons, and pre-booking momentum.

Phase IV

Opening & Stewardship

Directing on-site experience staging, operational rituals, and ongoing retainer-level design governance.
