Engagements

Three ways to work with us

We work at three depths: counsel when you need judgment, a project when you need a place planned and opened, a retainer when you need the plan kept. Each starts from the same ground.

Every property reaches a point where it needs something different from the people around it. Sometimes that is one sharp opinion. Sometimes it is the whole arc, from the first question to opening night. Sometimes it is someone who stays. The three engagements below are those three moments.

Choose by where your property stands today, not by how large you hope it becomes. The engagements are built to connect, so you can begin with counsel and move into a project, or move from a project into stewardship. The thinking carries over. That is the point of holding it in one place.

On-call counsel for a venue or business that needs expert judgment without a full engagement.

Best when you already have momentum and need a trusted outside eye on the decisions that shape the place.

Choosing

Not sure where to begin

Three questions usually settle it. Or take the five-minute readiness check.

  1. Do you know what the place should become, and for whom? If not, you need the whole arc. Tier II, Project
  2. Is the place already open, or the plan already set, and you need judgment on a few decisions? Tier I, Advisory
  3. Do you have several properties and worry that they will drift apart? Tier III, Retainer

Questions

What owners usually ask

Can we start with one engagement and move to another? {{icon:close}}

Yes. The work is built to connect. Counsel can grow into a project, and a project into stewardship, without starting the thinking over.

Who works on our engagement? {{icon:close}}

A collective of specialists matched to your property, not assigned from a bench. Which specialists join depends on what the work needs.

What happens first? {{icon:close}}

A diagnostic. We read what you already hold in assets, relationships, materials and timelines before we propose anything.

Do you work with a single property? {{icon:close}}

Yes. A single venue can begin with Advisory or a Project. A portfolio is where the Retainer earns its place.

How do we talk about scope and fees? {{icon:close}}

After the diagnostic session. Nothing is quoted on this site, because the right shape depends on what we find. Discussions are held under strict advisory confidentiality.

How is payment handled? {{icon:close}}

We accept card payments. Retainers are billed monthly. Project terms are set in the written agreement.

What size of project do you take on? {{icon:close}}

Most of our work is on projects from under a million dollars to about five million. If yours sits outside that, write to us and we will say honestly whether we are the right fit.
