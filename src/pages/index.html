What we do

Four phases, one arc

Most firms handle one piece: the brand, the build, the launch, the party. A place does not live in one piece. Juniper holds the whole arc, from the question of what a place should become, and for whom, to the morning it opens.

See the four phases {{icon:arrow-right}}

{{icon:compass}}Phase I

Conception & positioning

The core commercial ethos, the differentiation of each property, and an initial architectural thesis, set before ground is broken.

{{icon:swatch}}Phase II

Identity & pricing architecture

Yield modeling, visual systems, spatial vernacular and tiered guest segmentation, so that price and identity say the same thing.

{{icon:signal}}Phase III

Pre-launch demand

Private interest lists, editorial narrative, founder salons and pre-booking momentum, built while the place is still an idea.

{{icon:door}}Phase IV

Opening & stewardship

On-site experience staging, operational rituals and retainer-level design governance, so the opening holds and the intent outlasts it.

One world

A portfolio is a single argument made in many places

Three buildings on one clearing can be three different buildings, or one place seen from three sides. The difference is decided before anything is drawn.

That decision is the portfolio thesis: one organizing design that makes separate properties read as one world, then lets each property take its own local form.
