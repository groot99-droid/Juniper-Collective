How we work

Six steps, in the order a place needs them

From the first audit to the retainer that follows opening, each step leaves behind exactly what the next one needs, so nothing is decided twice and nothing is paid for twice.

Why this order

The order is not a preference

Set prices before the plan and you get a rate card with no reason behind it. Run a campaign before the identity and you advertise a place that does not exist yet. So we begin with what you hold, find the one idea that organizes it, and only then work out each property.

Every step depends on the one before it. The diagnostic shapes the thesis, the thesis shapes each property, the properties shape the story told before launch, and that story sets what the opening has to deliver.

Six steps carry your intent from the first conversation to the first guest without being rewritten on the way.

01
Discovery & audit

Diagnostic

We start with what already exists: the assets, relationships, materials and timelines you have in hand. Nothing is designed yet. We read your position honestly, name what is strong and what is unresolved, and agree what the thesis will have to answer. A diagnostic is not a delay; it is the ground the rest is built on.

What you receive

  • {{icon:check}}A written audit of assets and constraints
  • {{icon:check}}A map of open questions
  • {{icon:check}}A shared timeline

What we ask of you

  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Existing materials, plans and timelines
  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Introductions to key relationships
02
Conceptual synthesis

Portfolio thesis

One organizing design that makes separate properties read as one world. We gather what the diagnostic found and reduce it to a single idea strong enough to govern every later decision.

Not a slogan. Not a mood board. A thesis you can test a decision against.

What you receive

  • {{icon:check}}The Portfolio Thesis
  • {{icon:check}}A statement of design principles
  • {{icon:check}}A decision test for later choices

What we ask of you

  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Your response to draft theses
  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Your sign-off on the final thesis
03
Node specifications

Brand and audience by node

Each property receives its own mission, identity, price band and guest profile, specified so that it is distinct and still unmistakably part of the portfolio. The thesis is the constant. The node is where it takes local form.

What you receive

  • {{icon:check}}A specification for each property
  • {{icon:check}}A price band and guest profile per property
  • {{icon:check}}Identity direction per property

What we ask of you

  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Property facts such as site, scale and stage
  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Decisions on price positioning
04
Market cultivation

Pre-launch

Renders, stories and campaigns that build demand before the property exists. We make the place visible while it is still unbuilt, and we put it in front of the people it was designed for, so that opening day confirms an expectation rather than starting one.

What you receive

  • {{icon:check}}Renderings of the property
  • {{icon:check}}Campaign narrative and assets
  • {{icon:check}}A private interest list

What we ask of you

  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Access to founders and stakeholders for the story
  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Approval of public-facing material
05
Operational reality

Opening

The experience product, built and ready on day one. We stage what the guest meets first, and we make sure operations can reproduce it every day without the people who designed it. An experience that depends on its designers being in the room is not yet a product.

What you receive

  • {{icon:check}}An opening experience plan
  • {{icon:check}}Operational rituals for your team
  • {{icon:check}}A readiness review

What we ask of you

  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Your operating team's time in rehearsal
  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Access to the site before opening
06
Ongoing retainer

Stewardship

A retainer that protects the original intent as operations settle in. Staff change, pressures arrive, shortcuts offer themselves. We stay to hold decisions against the thesis, so the place you opened is still the place you designed.

What you receive

  • {{icon:check}}Design governance reviews
  • {{icon:check}}Refinement of operational rituals
  • {{icon:check}}A running decision log in your client portal

What we ask of you

  • {{icon:arrow-right}}Notice of changes before they are made
  • {{icon:arrow-right}}A standing place for us in key decisions

The deliverables shown are typical. The exact list for your engagement is agreed in its scope.

Why we stay

Why we stay after the doors open

Opening day tests a design once; operations test it every day after. That is why the work does not end at the doors.

Intent does not survive operations by accident; it survives because someone is charged with keeping it.
