Privacy & data security

What this site does with your information

Very little, by design. This page says exactly what.

Draft. This page describes how the site works today. It is a plain-language draft and has not yet been reviewed by counsel. Last updated October 2026.

In short

  • The forms on this site do not send or store anything. They open your own email app with a message addressed to us.
  • The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Email you send us is held in advisory confidence and used to respond to you.

What the site itself keeps

When you play the opening introduction, your browser remembers that it has played, for the length of that browser session only, so it does not repeat on every page. This is a single yes-or-no marker stored in your browser. It contains no personal information and is cleared when you close the tab.

Third parties the site relies on

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub receives technical request data such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The type on this site is loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests it from Google and Google receives your IP address, under Google's privacy policy.

When you email us

If you write to inquiries@junipercollective.co, we receive what you send, including your email address and anything you choose to tell us about your property or portfolio. We use it to reply to you and to prepare for any conversation that follows. We do not sell it or share it for marketing. Discussions are treated under strict advisory confidentiality.

Client portal

Access to a client portal is issued as part of an engagement and is governed by the written agreement for that engagement, which sets out how engagement documents and decisions are stored, who can see them, and how they are returned or deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to the address above. Where a law gives you further rights, those apply as well.

Changes

If the site starts to do more than is described here, for example by adding analytics, this page will change first and the date above will change with it.
