Terms of engagement

How an engagement is set up

The short version: a written agreement governs the work. This site only describes it.

Draft. This is a plain-language outline of how engagements work. It is not a contract and has not yet been reviewed by counsel. The agreement you sign for an engagement is the binding document. Last updated October 2026.

This website

The pages on this site describe how Juniper Collective works. They are information, not advice, and nothing on them is a quote, an offer or a promise of any result. Fees are not published here; they are discussed after the diagnostic session.

A written agreement governs every engagement

Advisory, Project and Retainer engagements each begin with a written agreement that sets out the scope, the people involved, the fees, the timing and the ownership of the work. Where anything on this site differs from that agreement, the agreement governs.

Confidentiality

Discussions are treated under strict advisory confidentiality. What you tell us about your property, your portfolio and your plans is used for your engagement and is not shared outside it without your agreement. The written agreement sets out the detail, including how engagement documents are held in the client portal.

The diagnostic

An exploratory diagnostic session or intake review is the first step. It is a conversation about what you are building and where it stands. It does not by itself create an engagement or any obligation on either side.

Fees and payment

Fees are discussed after the diagnostic and set out in the written agreement. Card payments are accepted. Retainers are billed monthly. Project fees follow the schedule in the agreement.

The client portal

Portal access is issued through an engagement and is for the people named on it. Access can be added for members of your team by asking us.

Work product

Who owns what we make, and what you may do with it, is set by the written agreement for the engagement.
