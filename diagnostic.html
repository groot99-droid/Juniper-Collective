The diagnostic

Begin with what you already hold

Before we propose anything, we read what exists: your assets, relationships, materials and timelines. The diagnostic is the first phase of every engagement, and the first session of this one.

Request a diagnostic

What it is

A careful reading, done before any design begins

The diagnostic is discovery and audit. It is a careful reading of everything already in your hands.

It comes first because a plan built on an assumption inherits the assumption. Most owners hold more than they realize: a site, a network, a half-formed brand, a calendar already set in motion. Until that is laid out in one place, every later decision is made partly blind.

The result is not a verdict. It is a clear ground to build from, and an honest account of what the place could become.

Assets

The property, the land, the spaces and the capital already committed, read for what they can carry.

Relationships

The partners, the people and the community around the place, and what each could mean to it.

Materials

The plans, drawings, brand files and finishes already made, and how far they agree with each other.

Timelines

The dates already promised, to lenders, builders and guests, and what they leave room for.

What to bring

  • Plans and drawings
  • Pro formas or financial outlines
  • Brand files and existing identity work
  • Schedules and key dates
  • Names of key contacts and partners

Bring what you have. Nothing needs to be finished.

What you leave with

  • A plain account of what you hold
  • The questions that still decide the outcome
  • A view of what the place could become, and for whom
  • A recommendation on which engagement fits
