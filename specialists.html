Specialists

A collective, matched to the engagement

We do not assign from a bench. For each engagement we bring together the disciplines your portfolio needs, and you work with them directly.

A collective, not a bench

The person across the table is there because your problem needs them

A bench is a list of people who are available. A collective is a set of people who are right. You can tell the difference in the first week.

Picture a table set for one particular dinner, not a banquet hall. Five disciplines can sit at it: economics, culinary, brand, systems and space. Which seats are filled depends on what you are building.

Intent is specific, and generic expertise dilutes it.

Five disciplines

Who can sit at the table

Economics & experience architecture

The business structure of a hospitality venture, the logic of its pricing and the design of the guest journey are one system, not three. This discipline makes them agree, so the experience you design is one the economics can carry.

  • Pricing logic and yield modeling
  • Business structure review
  • Guest journey mapping

Culinary & kitchen management

A menu is a statement of the place, and a kitchen is where that statement is kept or broken. This discipline shapes the concept, the menu strategy and the operational readiness behind them.

  • Food concept development
  • Menu strategy
  • Kitchen readiness review

Brand, marketing & product design

Identity, rendering, storytelling and campaign: the layer through which the world first meets the place. It is built outward from the thesis, so that what is shown matches what is delivered.

  • Identity systems
  • Renderings and visual storytelling
  • Campaign development

Systems & communications

The tools your team and ours use to stay aligned. A decision that cannot be found later has not really been made, so this discipline keeps the record, the documents and the conversation in one place.

  • Client portal setup
  • Decision and document records
  • Team communication workflow

Spatial curation & environmental production

Specifications for how a place sounds, looks and feels to be in: acoustic, lighting and site activation. Space is where the thesis becomes physical, and where a guest first decides whether to believe it.

  • Acoustic specifications
  • Lighting specifications
  • Site activation plans

The lists show the kinds of work each discipline typically takes on. Your engagement's scope sets the exact work.
